## Overview

Scams now target every age group, and the design response has largely been more warnings. More warnings produce less reading.

This concept starts from a different question: **what does intervention look like when the person cannot be persuaded in the moment?**

## Coming soon

This case study is in development. It will cover the research framing, the intervention model, and the accessibility and dignity constraints that shape it.
